Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best time to visit Florida?

Florida is great year-round! For comfortable weather and fewer crowds, consider visiting between March-May or September-November.

Do I need a car to get around?

While public transportation exists in major cities, having a car gives you the most flexibility to explore Florida's diverse attractions.

What should I pack for Florida?

Light, breathable clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, and comfortable shoes. Don't forget a light jacket for air-conditioned spaces!

Are theme parks the only attractions?

Not at all! Florida offers beautiful beaches, natural springs, the Everglades, historic sites, world-class museums, and vibrant cultural scenes.
