Salt Lake City

Located along the Wasatch Mountains in the northern part of Utah is Salt Lake City. Salt Lake City is Utahs capitol and most populated city with approximately 209,593 people. Founded in 1847 it is also Utah's oldest city. Even with it's humble beginnings Salt Lake City has grown into an urban metropolis.

With so many people there are many job opportunities in Salt Lake City. The average annual income is $61,766, which is considerably higher than the states $49,019 average.
