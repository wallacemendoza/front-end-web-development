Florida Beaches

Miles of pristine coastline where paradise meets reality

Sun, Sand & Sea

With over 1,300 miles of coastline, Florida boasts some of the world's most beautiful beaches. From the sugar-white sands of the Gulf Coast to the vibrant Atlantic shores, Florida's beaches offer something for everyone - whether you seek relaxation, adventure, or natural beauty.

Crystal-clear turquoise waters, gentle waves, and year-round sunshine make Florida's beaches the perfect escape. Each stretch of sand has its own personality, from bustling family-friendly shores to secluded tropical paradises.

Plan Your Visit

Beach Tips & Essentials

Best Time to Visit

March through May offers warm weather with fewer crowds. Summer is peak season with hot temperatures and occasional afternoon storms. Fall and winter provide pleasant temperatures, though water may be cooler.

What to Bring

Don't forget: sunscreen (SPF 30+), hat, sunglasses, beach towel, water bottle, beach umbrella or tent, snacks, and a waterproof bag for valuables. Many beaches have rentals available for chairs and umbrellas.

Beach Safety

Always swim near a lifeguard station, check beach flags for conditions, stay hydrated, reapply sunscreen frequently, and be aware of rip currents. Respect wildlife and never feed animals.

Parking & Access

Many popular beaches have paid parking ($5-$10/day). Arrive early during peak season to secure a spot. Some beaches offer free parking or public access points with limited spaces.
