State Parks

Explore Florida's pristine natural treasures and outdoor adventures

Nature's Playground

Florida's state parks protect some of the most diverse and beautiful ecosystems in the United States. From crystal-clear springs and ancient forests to coastal dunes and mangrove swamps, these natural sanctuaries offer endless opportunities for adventure, wildlife viewing, and outdoor recreation.

With 175 state parks and trails covering over 800,000 acres, Florida's state park system preserves unique habitats and provides access to incredible natural wonders. Whether you're seeking hiking, camping, kayaking, or simply a peaceful escape into nature, Florida's state parks deliver.

Top Parks

Must-Visit State Parks

🌿

Everglades National Park

South Florida

The largest tropical wilderness in the U.S., home to rare and endangered species like manatees, American crocodiles, and Florida panthers. Explore by airboat, kayak, or hiking trail.

Wildlife Airboat Tours Unique Ecosystem
💧

Ichetucknee Springs

North Florida

Crystal-clear springs perfect for tubing and swimming. Float down the pristine river surrounded by lush vegetation and observe fish and turtles in the clear water below.

Tubing Swimming Clear Springs
🏕️

Bahia Honda State Park

Florida Keys

Pristine beaches, excellent snorkeling, and stunning views of the old Bahia Honda Rail Bridge. One of the Keys' most beautiful natural areas with abundant marine life.

Beaches Snorkeling Camping
🦅

Myakka River State Park

Sarasota

One of Florida's oldest and largest parks, featuring diverse wildlife, hiking trails, and a canopy walkway offering treetop views of the wetlands below.

Hiking Wildlife Canopy Walk
🌺

John Pennekamp Coral Reef

Key Largo

America's first underwater park, protecting living coral reefs. Snorkel or dive among colorful tropical fish, sea turtles, and the famous Christ of the Abyss statue.

Diving Coral Reefs Marine Life
🌲

Wakulla Springs State Park

Tallahassee Area

Home to one of the world's largest and deepest freshwater springs. Take a riverboat tour to see alligators, birds, and manatees in their natural habitat.

Springs Boat Tours Manatees

Plan Your Visit

State Park Tips

Park Entrance Fees

Most state parks charge $2-$10 per vehicle entrance fee. Consider purchasing an annual pass for $60 (individual) or $120 (family) if you plan multiple visits. Passes cover entrance to all Florida state parks.

What to Bring

Pack sunscreen, insect repellent, plenty of water, snacks, appropriate footwear, binoculars for wildlife viewing, and a camera. Don't forget your swimsuit for parks with springs or beaches!

Best Times to Visit

Fall through spring (October-April) offers the most comfortable weather for hiking and outdoor activities. Summer can be hot and humid with afternoon thunderstorms. Early morning visits offer the best wildlife viewing opportunities.

Safety & Etiquette

Stay on designated trails, never feed wildlife, carry out all trash, respect posted rules and closures, and be aware of wildlife like alligators and snakes. Always let someone know your plans when hiking or camping.
