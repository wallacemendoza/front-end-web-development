Top 3 State Parks in Florida

  1. Myakka River State Park

    This sprawling park offers diverse ecosystems, including prairies, forests, and wetlands.

  2. Dry Tortugas National Park

    Located in the Florida Keys, this remote park is accessible only by boat or seaplane.

  3. Everglades National Park

    This vast wilderness area is home to a unique ecosystem of sawgrass marshes, mangrove forests, and the iconic Florida Everglades.
