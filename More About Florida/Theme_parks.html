Top 3 Theme Parks in Florida

  1. Walt Disney World

    A extensive complex featuring four theme parks: Magic Kingdom, Epcot, Disney's Hollywood Studios, and Disney's Animal Kingdom.

  2. Universal Orlando

    Home to two incredible theme parks: Universal Studios Florida and Universal's Islands of Adventure.Offers a unique blend of cinematic experiences and thrilling adventures.

  3. Sea World Orlando

    Home of amazing marine lifes, thrilling rides, and entertaining shows featuring animals like dolphins, whales, and penguins.
