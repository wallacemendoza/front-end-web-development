Theme Parks

World-class thrills, magic, and unforgettable family adventures

The Theme Park Capital of the World

Florida is home to more theme parks than any other destination on Earth. From the enchantment of Disney World to the movie magic of Universal Studios, Florida's theme parks set the global standard for entertainment, innovation, and immersive experiences.

Each year, millions of visitors from around the world come to Florida to experience cutting-edge attractions, meet beloved characters, enjoy world-class shows, and create lifelong memories. Whether you're seeking thrilling roller coasters or magical storytelling, Florida's theme parks deliver it all.

Plan Your Visit

Theme Park Tips

Best Times to Visit

Avoid peak crowds by visiting during off-peak seasons (January-February, September-October, excluding holidays). Weekdays are typically less crowded than weekends. Arrive at park opening for shortest wait times.

Tickets & Multi-Day Passes

Multi-day tickets offer better value than single-day passes. Consider park hopper options to visit multiple parks in one day. Purchase tickets in advance online for discounts and to guarantee admission.

Fast Pass & Skip the Line

Utilize Lightning Lane, Express Pass, or Fast Pass systems to minimize wait times on popular attractions. Book these as early as possible. Single Rider lines can also save significant time.

What to Bring

Pack sunscreen, comfortable walking shoes, portable phone charger, refillable water bottle, rain poncho, and snacks. Download park apps for real-time wait times, maps, and mobile food ordering.
