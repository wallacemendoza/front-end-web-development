Miami: The Magic City

Miami, a vibrant metropolis, offers a unique blend of Latin American culture, stunning beaches, and a dynamic arts scene. Explore the iconic Art Deco district, soak up the sun on South Beach, or experience the vibrant nightlife in Wynwood.

Miami is a melting pot of cultures, offering diverse culinary experiences, from Cuban cafes to international cuisine. Immerse yourself in the city's rich history, visit world-class museums, or simply enjoy the laid-back beach lifestyle.

Whether you're seeking sun, culture, or excitement, Miami has something to offer every visitor.

Fun Facts About Miami

  • Nickname: The Magic City
  • Founded: 1896
  • Population: Approximately 470,000
  • Region: South Florida
  • Classification: Urban
  • Avg. income of the city compared to the rest of the state: 20% higher
  • Known for: Beaches, Art Deco, Latin Culture
