Orlando

The Theme Park Capital of the World

Welcome to Orlando

Orlando is a vibrant city in Central Florida, world-renowned as the theme park capital of the globe. Home to Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, and countless other attractions, Orlando draws millions of visitors each year seeking magical experiences and unforgettable adventures.

Beyond the theme parks, Orlando boasts a thriving arts and culture scene, beautiful lakes, diverse dining options, and year-round sunshine. Whether you're seeking thrills, relaxation, or family fun, Orlando has something for everyone.

Top Attractions

Must-Visit Places in Orlando

🏰

Walt Disney World

The most magical place on Earth! Explore four theme parks: Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, and Animal Kingdom.

🎬

Universal Studios

Experience movie magic with thrilling rides, including The Wizarding World of Harry Potter and exciting attractions based on your favorite films.

🐬

SeaWorld Orlando

Marine life meets thrilling rides. Encounter amazing sea creatures and enjoy world-class roller coasters and shows.

🎨

International Drive

Orlando's entertainment hub featuring restaurants, shopping, the Icon Orlando observation wheel, and countless attractions.

🌳

Lake Eola Park

A beautiful urban park in downtown Orlando with swan boat rentals, walking paths, and the iconic fountain centerpiece.

🍽️

Restaurant Row

Sand Lake Road offers an incredible variety of world-class dining experiences, from upscale steakhouses to international cuisine.
