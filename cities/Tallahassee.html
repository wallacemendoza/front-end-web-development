Tallahassee: The Capital City

Tallahassee, the capital of Florida, offers a unique blend of history, culture, and Southern charm. Explore the historic Capitol Building, visit the Florida State University campus, or wander through the beautiful natural areas surrounding the city.

Tallahassee boasts a vibrant arts scene, with numerous museums, galleries, and live music venues. Enjoy the city's relaxed atmosphere, diverse dining options, and friendly Southern hospitality.

Whether you're a history buff, an outdoor enthusiast, or simply seeking a peaceful getaway, Tallahassee has something to offer every visitor.

Fun Facts About Tallahassee

  • Nickname: Tally
  • Founded: 1821
  • Population: Approximately 202,221
  • Region: Northwest (Florida Panhandle)
  • Classification: Urban
  • Avg. income of the city compared to the rest of the state: 10% lower
  • Known for: State Capital, College Town, History & Culture
