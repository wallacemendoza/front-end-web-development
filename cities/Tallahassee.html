City Spotlight

Tallahassee

Florida's Capital City - Where History Meets Southern Charm

Welcome to Tallahassee

Tallahassee, Florida's capital city, is a unique blend of government, education, and Southern charm. Nestled in the panhandle among rolling hills and canopy roads, Tallahassee offers a different side of Florida - one steeped in history, culture, and natural beauty.

Home to Florida State University and Florida A&M University, Tallahassee has a vibrant college-town atmosphere alongside its role as the state capital. From historic plantations to pristine natural springs, Tallahassee combines political significance with outdoor adventure and cultural richness.

Top Attractions

Must-Visit Places in Tallahassee

🏛️

Florida State Capitol

Tour the historic and modern capitol buildings. The 22-story New Capitol offers stunning views from the observation deck overlooking the city.

🌳

Canopy Roads

Drive or bike under majestic oak trees draped in Spanish moss along Tallahassee's scenic historic canopy roads - a truly enchanting experience.

💧

Wakulla Springs

One of the world's largest and deepest freshwater springs. Take a riverboat tour to see alligators, birds, and pristine natural beauty.

🏺

Mission San Luis

Experience 17th-century Spanish colonial and Apalachee Indian life at this living history museum with reconstructed buildings and artifacts.

🏈

Doak Campbell Stadium

Home of the FSU Seminoles! Experience the excitement of college football and the passionate game-day atmosphere in Tallahassee.

🎨

Railroad Square Art District

A creative hub featuring art galleries, studios, vintage shops, and unique eateries in repurposed railroad warehouses.

Why Tallahassee

What Makes Tallahassee Special

01

Rich History

The only Confederate state capital not captured during the Civil War, with preserved historic districts and antebellum architecture.

02

Natural Beauty

Rolling hills, crystal-clear springs, and majestic canopy roads create a landscape unique in Florida.

03

College Town Energy

Home to two major universities, bringing youthful energy, cultural events, and championship sports teams.

04

Southern Hospitality

Experience genuine Southern charm, friendly locals, and a slower pace of life while still enjoying urban amenities.
